How to Track MMO Gold, Crafting Cooldowns and Auction Profit in a Spreadsheet

Set up a gold log, a cooldown timer and an auction sales sheet that work out net and profit after fees, for every character on your account, in Google Sheets or Excel.

If you play an online game with several characters, the question "am I actually making gold?" is hard to answer from memory. Gold moves between alts, crafting materials cost gold before the item sells, and the auction house takes a cut. A spreadsheet answers it in three tables: a log of every gold change, a list of crafting cooldowns, and a list of auction sales with the fee and material cost taken off. Here is how to build that from a blank sheet, then a ready-made version.

Step 1: A Characters tab with a starting balance

One row per character: name, server, main or alt, and starting gold, the amount it had on the day you began tracking. You never type current gold again; the sheet works it out as starting gold plus everything logged since. If you type current gold by hand, the log and the balance will drift apart within a week.

Step 2: A Gold log

One row per gold change that is not an auction sale: quest rewards, loot sold to a vendor, repairs, materials bought, gold sent to an alt. Columns: date, character, category, amount, note. Amount is positive for gold in and negative for gold out. A transfer between two of your characters is two rows, minus on one and plus on the other, so the total across the account does not change.

Add a helper column for the month, =DATE(YEAR(A2),MONTH(A2),1), so you can total by month later. Use data validation to make the character column a dropdown from the Characters tab; typos in names are what break SUMIFS.

Step 3: Crafting cooldowns

One row per cooldown: character, recipe, cooldown hours, last used (date and time). Then:

Ready at   = last_used + cooldown_hours / 24
Status     = IF(ready_at <= NOW(), "Ready", "Waiting")
Hours left = MAX(0, ROUND((ready_at - NOW()) * 24, 1))

NOW() only updates when the sheet recalculates (any edit, or F9 in Excel), so Hours left is a snapshot, not a live clock. That is fine for a glance before logging in.

Step 4: Auction sales with fee and profit

One row per listing: date listed, character, item, quantity, price each, material cost for the whole row, status (Listed, Sold, Expired, Cancelled), date sold. Put your game's auction fee in one cell as a fraction (0.05 for 5 percent) and refer to it, so changing it later fixes every row.

Sale total = quantity * price_each
Fee        = IF(status="Sold", ROUND(sale_total * fee, 2), "")
Net        = IF(status="Sold", sale_total - fee, "")
Profit     = IF(status="Sold", net - material_cost, "")

Only Sold rows count. Expired and Cancelled rows stay as a record but add nothing.

Do not count materials twice. If you bought the materials, log the purchase on the Gold log as a negative amount (that is real gold leaving). The material cost column on the sales tab is only for working out profit; it must not feed into the balance.

Step 5: Current gold and a summary

On the Characters tab:

Gold log total = SUMIFS(GoldLog!Amount, GoldLog!Character, A2)
Auction net    = SUMIFS(Sales!Net, Sales!Character, A2, Sales!Status, "Sold")
Current gold   = starting_gold + gold_log_total + auction_net

Then a Summary tab with total gold across all characters, gold in and gold out, auction sales total, fees paid and profit, average profit per sale, and a 12 month table using the month helper column. A bar chart of current gold per character shows at a glance which alt is the bank.

FAQ

How do I get the .xlsx into Google Sheets?

In Google Drive, New, File upload, open the file, then File, Save as Google Sheets. Set your time zone under File, Settings so the cooldown clock is right.

Why do the formulas show blank in Excel?

A yellow "Protected View" bar at the top means Excel has not calculated yet. Click "Enable Editing".

My game has a listing deposit on expired auctions. Where does that go?

On the Gold log as a negative amount with category Other. The fee cell only covers the percentage cut on sales.

Can I track more than one game?

Make a copy of the file per game. Fees and cooldowns differ, so one file per game is cleaner than a game column.
