JRPG Game Music Pack: 10 Loops

10 instrumental fantasy RPG loops (battle, town, dungeon, menu and more) as WAV and OGG. Pay what you want, $8 minimum.

Ten instrumental loops for fantasy role-playing games. Two battle themes (a regular fight and a boss), town, village, shop, overworld, travel, dungeon, menu and a quiet rest theme. Each loop runs between 45 and 140 seconds.

Every file is exactly one loop. It starts on a beat and the last 0.12 seconds are crossfaded into the music that came before, so when your engine jumps from the end back to the start the music carries on. I checked every join by script on both the WAV and the OGG. Turn on loop for the whole clip in Godot, Unity, GameMaker or RPG Maker and you are done.

In the zip: 10 WAV files (44.1 kHz, 16-bit, stereo), the same 10 as OGG, tracks.json with title, length, BPM, key and loudness for each track, a README and the license. All tracks sit near -14 LUFS.

Limits: instrumental only, no stingers or victory fanfares, and a single loop will repeat if the player stays long. BPM and key are measured and can be off by a step.

Made with AI tools and edited by hand. The music comes from Stable Audio 3 Medium (Powered by Stability AI) running on my own computer. I listened to every track and threw out the bad takes; code did the looping and leveling. Use in any commercial or non-commercial project, no credit needed. You may not resell the tracks, register them with Content ID, or use them to train AI models.
