One console only: the retail games released for the Super Nintendo Entertainment System and the Super Famicom, 1990 to 2000, as listed on English Wikipedia. 1,749 rows, one per game, 19 fields, as CSV and JSON.

Fields: title, other regional titles, developers, a publisher and release date for each of Japan, North America and PAL, the regions a game came out in, first release date, year and region, genres, game modes, series, Wikidata ID and Wikipedia article.

Coverage: 1,440 Japanese, 717 North American and 528 PAL releases. 1,179 rows are joined to Wikidata; 1,084 have a genre.

Sources are public: the English Wikipedia list of the console's games (CC BY-SA 4.0) and Wikidata (CC0). The data is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0, so you may use it commercially and share it, with credit and under the same license. What you pay for is the cleaning, the region split, the date normalization, the Wikidata join, a JSON Schema, and a planned quarterly refresh.

Limits: retail games only, with no unlicensed, promotional or aftermarket games. 665 rows (38%) have no genre, most of them Japan-only games. Dates keep the precision the source gives (a year, a month or a full day). Community-edited sources can be wrong.
