About and privacy

ComradeCoding makes small data tools, printables and templates. Each one does one job, is tested before release, and is sold through an established store (Apify, Gumroad or itch.io), which handles payment, delivery and refunds.

Refunds: if a digital product does not work for you, ask within 30 days through the store you bought it from and you get your money back.

Privacy: this site has no cookies, no analytics, no trackers and no ads, and it collects nothing about you. The stores we link to have their own privacy policies.

Some product text and content was written with AI (Claude) and checked by a person; each product page says so where it applies.
